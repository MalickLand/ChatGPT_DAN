## MLS / Zillow Compliance Rules

MLS boards and Zillow require that video and ads show **only the subject property and its deeded land**.

### DO USE
- Photos/video of the actual Advent Dr lot (provided by client)
- Virtual twilight edits of the real cabin
- Text overlays: price, acreage, features
- MalickLand logo
- Drone-style cinematic motion from stills of the actual lot

### DO NOT USE
- Stock video (deer, generic mountains, other cabins)
- Renderings of a future/unbuilt house
- Neighboring properties or views not on the 3.11 acres
- Actors or lifestyle shots unless physically on-site
