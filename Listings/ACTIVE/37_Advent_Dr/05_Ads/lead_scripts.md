## Objection Responses

**"Price is too high"**
"I hear you. What were you budgeted at? Let me see what we can do — there may be room to work depending on terms."

**"I need to think about it"**
"Totally fair. What specifically do you want to think through? Happy to help you work through it now so you've got everything you need."

**"I need to talk to my spouse / partner"**
"Of course. Do you want me to put together a one-pager with the highlights you can show them? Makes the conversation easier."

**"Is this in a flood zone?"**
"Good question — I have the flood map documentation. Let me send that over right now."
